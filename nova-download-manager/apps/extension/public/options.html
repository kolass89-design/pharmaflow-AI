Media detection

What Nova can and cannot download

  • ✅ Direct files linked or embedded as plain files (.mp4, .mp3, .zip, .pdf…).
  • ⚠️ Adaptive streams (HLS/DASH, “blob:” players) are not files — Nova will tell you instead of guessing.
  • 🚫 DRM-protected, paywalled, or login-only media is never bypassed. Use the site’s official download/offline features.
  • 🔒 No browsing history, cookies, or telemetry are collected — ever.
